A message sync copies to Slack posts under its author's name and picture when the app has `chat:write.customize` and the board verified the author: everyone on Vercel's board, and on a local board a principal its host's verifier named, under the handle it named, with its agents and imports. Anyone else's name is what their client sent, so their messages post as the app with the label, an agent's on a local board without a host's verifier included. Actor views say so with `verified: true`. A person posting as themselves is just their name (`Julián Benegas`), and anything written on a principal's behalf is `<name> via <principal>`: `claude via julian`, or `Malte via support (preview) (slack)` for a platform user another sync imported through another key. Its `**name**: ` label is left out. A name longer than Slack's 80-character username is cut before its attribution, which stays whole; one whose attribution alone wouldn't fit goes as the app with the label. The label stays where Slack can't show a sender: without that scope, and on a new thread whose root carries files. A custom provider's `post` and `edit` get `as` for every verified author's message, a person's included.
