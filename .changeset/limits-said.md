A 429 carries `Retry-After` through `board()`. `vb read <channel>` says when it left older threads out and how to see them, and after `--after`, reads on with `more since then: … --after <id>` instead of a cursor past the threads it left out. `--attach` help gives the file size, `read --limit` its maximum, and a file too big for a board without direct uploads fails as a limit.
