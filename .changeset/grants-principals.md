Grants name principals. A grant is `{ id, principals, can }`: who it names, as `user`, `project`, `github`, `oidc`, `all-users`, `all-projects`, or `everyone`, and one `can` for all of them, a map from channel pattern to permissions, `[]` for none. `team.createGrant({ principals: [{ kind: 'everyone' }], can: { 'security/': [] } })` closes `security/`, and `team.createGrant({ principals: [{ kind: 'user', email: 'alice@acme.com' }], can: { 'security/': 'all' } })` opens it to Alice alone. For a caller and a channel, the most specific pattern among the grants that name it wins, then the closest principal (one principal, then a group, then `everyone`), and grants tied there add up. `everyone` lets no one in by itself. `access`'s rules meet the grants as principals of their kind. `team.updateGrant(id, { add, remove, can })` (`PATCH /grants/:id`) changes a grant by its ID.

Every board starts with g1, every person with everything, and g2, every project on the board's team in production, preview, and development with read, post, react, and label everywhere, as Vercel's API seeds a team's board. You can't grant what you don't have: your token's and your own grants' limits count, an `everyone` lock doesn't, and anyone you let in gets what `everyone` gets. Revoking, or removing from a grant, takes `manage` at its patterns. Naming an `oidc` principal takes `manage` everywhere. `checkGrantChange` in `@vercel/board/access` is the rule, with no I/O, so a backend that keeps grants runs the same one. Listing grants takes `manage` at `*`. Owners always may: the team's on Vercel's API, and on a local board its local user, the one `vb serve` mints a token for. A grant's keys are strict, and two oidc issuers that name the same principals are refused.

Breaking: `{ issuer, claims, can? }` grants are gone, with setting a grant again to change its `can`, and `createGrant` answers `{ grant }` without `created`. A local board folds the grants it kept before into nothing. A rule like `vercelProject({ project: 'prj_front', can: { 'feedback/': ['post'] } })` no longer keeps a project on the team out of the rest of the board, since g2's `*` lets it in: write `{ '*': [], 'feedback/': ['post'] }`, or revoke g2. `vb grants create` is gone until it takes principals, and `vb grants` lists each grant's principals as the flags that name them. Custom backends implement `updateGrant`.
