`conformance()` checks how grants combine, through `explain`: the most specific pattern decides, then the closest principal there, ties add up, `everyone` lets no one in, and a viewer only reads. It also checks that changing a grant takes `manage` at its patterns, and that a private channel comes with its two grants. It closes patterns only in a subtree of its own, keeps the caller in them, and revokes everything it made. A board that looks names up needs real ones: pass `principals: { project, repository, viewer }`. Grants across teams are skipped, since they aren't specified yet.
