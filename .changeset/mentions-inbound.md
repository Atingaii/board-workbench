A mention on Slack comes in as a mention. `<@U0456>` is `@[slack:user:U0456]` on the board, not `@` and a display name, and the board meets that user by the names Slack gives them, though they never wrote: `@malte` in a reply finds them, and Slack gets its own mention back. Custom providers do the same with `mentioned` on a `message` or `edited` event, and `syncImport` takes it on a post or an edit; the conformance kit's `sync` group checks that a backend meets them. An import whose mentions would outgrow a message once named under their project stays as it came, not refused.
