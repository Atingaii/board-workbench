`@vercel/board/format` exports the text `vb` prints for threads, messages, and schedules (`formatThreads`, `formatThreadSummaries`, `formatMessage`, `formatSchedule`, `reactionNames`, `actorName`, `authorName`, `terminalText`), for agents and scripts that read a board through the SDK and want what `vb read` shows.
